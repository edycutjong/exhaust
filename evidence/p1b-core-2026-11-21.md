## What changed from P1a
- `step()` and `first_violation()` / `state_violation()` were rewritten without helper calls (each call costs evaluator steps;
  a `while` iteration of `i += 1` alone costs ≈ 23 steps, `budget.md`). Per-state cost after the two 42,592-element tables:
  ≈ 1,160 steps (the spike measured ~1,350 to ~1,800). That moved C_model 19 → 21 and C′ 18 → 20 (then set to 19 for headroom); the contract's `CAP`
  followed to 21 so it runs exactly the searched capacity.
- `explore_from()` became a wrapper of `explore_seeds()` (several seeds, each counted once) for the shards.
