## What changed from P1a
- `step()` and `state_violation()` / `first_violation()` were rewritten without helper calls (each call costs evaluator steps;
  one `while` iteration of `i += 1` alone costs ≈ 23 steps, `budget.md`). Per-state cost after the two 42,592-element tables:
  ≈ 1,160 steps. That moved C_model 19 → 21 and the largest green trace capacity 18 → 20 (C′ pinned at 19 for headroom); the
  contract's `CAP` followed to 21 so it runs exactly the searched capacity.
- `explore_from()` became a wrapper of `explore_seeds()` (several seeds, each counted once) for the shards.
- The edge pins stop once a model passes STATES states and defer to `explore()`, so a buggy, larger graph does not add
  step-limit errors to the red build.
